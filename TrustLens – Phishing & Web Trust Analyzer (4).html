Paste a link, validate a certificate, analyse keys, assess headers and share threats, all in one place. Analysis runs in your browser.

🔗 URL scan
Detect phishing, typosquatting, homographs and malicious redirects.
 📄 Certificate
Check expiry, SAN match, weak algorithms, CT logging and CA watchlist.
 🔑 Key analysis
Parse PEM keys; find small factors, close primes, ROCA and shared primes.
 🛡️ Headers
Assess HSTS, CSP, cookie flags and outdated software.
 📣 Threat feed
Report malicious domains and cross-check scans against community reports.


Prototype takes certificate fields as input. The full build pulls them from the live TLS handshake and CT logs server-side.
